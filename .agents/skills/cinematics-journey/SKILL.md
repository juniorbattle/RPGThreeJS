# Cinematics and Journey

Contract: PRESENTATION_AND_MEDIA (`docs/contracts/PRESENTATION_AND_MEDIA.md`). Scope decisions: `docs/autonomy/OPERATOR_DECISIONS.md` (OD-2026-10-01-B). Maps: `docs/autonomy/CINEMATIC_CONVERSION_MATRIX.md` and `docs/autonomy/CINEMATIC_EIGHT_SLOT_REMASTER.md` (the generation continuation in the latter is superseded).

## The eight slots

`camp_departure`, `alaric_audience_arrival`, `bois_clair_arrival`, `bois_clair_saved`, `bois_clair_sacrificed`, `lion_judgement`, `serpent_route_ending`, `lion_trial_route_ending`. Source of truth in code: `src/cinematics/ApprovedProductionVideos.ts`. Anything else is drift, not a ninth slot. A slot ID says nothing about whether the current video is final art.

## Ownership

- Player and surfaces: `src/cinematics/` (`CinematicPlayer`, `CinematicOverlay`, `CinematicRegistry`, `CinematicTriggers`, `CinematicPreloader`, `CinematicReductionPolicy`, `CinematicDialogueSession`, `Journey*`, `TravelStillSurface`). Journey boundaries: `src/journey/`.
- Manifest: `public/assets/cinematics/manifest.json` holds eight MP4 descriptors plus the source-free `qa-placeholder`, which belongs to the isolated QA lab only.
- `FinalPresentationRegistry.generated.ts` and `FinalDialoguePresentation.generated.ts` are generated by `tools/cinematics/generate_cin6d6_runtime_registry.ts` from the immutable historical audit plus the overlay `tools/cinematics/specs/production_presentation_modes.json`. Never edit them by hand and never regenerate from the historical audit alone.
- Archive: the retired MP4s are byte-identical under `tools/cinematics/archive/retired-video-masters/` (hash inventory there). The 31-entry historical manifest is `tools/cinematics/specs/historical_cinematic_manifest_31.json`. Do not move them.
- Ignored candidates and masters under `tmp/cinematics/**` are evidence: never delete them.

## Runtime boundaries

Video owns its integrated cast during playback. Dialogue returns to STATIC_TABLEAU with the stage owning the cast. A hold is used only for the exact moment established by the preceding video and does not authorize dialogue or choices on frozen video. One primary surface at a time and 1 to 4 tableau actors. Fallback preserves agency and never replays resolved content. Historical trigger maps still name retired beats; `GameApp` blocks their playback.

## Scope limits for recurring runs

No generation or polling of video or keyframes (no MiniMax), no MP4 replacement or promotion, no acceptance of the six retained candidates. Media remaster is an external manual workstream. A prologue or first-refuge video is planning only: no new slot, ID, placeholder or media until a dedicated contract task extends the list.

## Structural-readiness checklist (queue item 8)

For each of the eight slots: canonical trigger; actor and surface ownership; transition in and out; return to tableau or dialogue; retained choices and outcomes; skip and release; reduced motion (game setting and OS-only); missing-media fallback; loading; save and resume. Reuse existing evidence before running new QA.

## Verify

- Focused: `npx vitest run src/cinematics src/journey` or the touched files, then `npx tsc --noEmit` and `npm run contracts:validate`.
- Media: `tools/cinematics/probe_production_eight_slots.mjs` and `tools/cinematics/validate_campaign_cinematic_census.mjs` (read each header first).
- Browser: `run_eight_slot_browser_qa.mjs`, `run_production_eight_slot_media_qa.mjs` and `run_cin6d6_route_browser_qa.mjs`; flags, ports and outputs are in the `qa-evidence` skill.
- After a build, `dist` holds exactly eight MP4s.

## Hand off

Staging and dialogue: `narrative-tableau`. Reduced motion, focus, viewports: `ui-accessibility`. Canon or save impact: `contracts-compliance`.

## Return (impact brief)

Contracts read; files and owners affected; invariants at risk (contract section and why); verification required (exact commands); cross-domain handoffs; verdict OK, OK with conditions, or BLOCKED.
