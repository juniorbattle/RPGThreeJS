## Operator answers (2026-10-29, America/Toronto)

**Trigger.** OD-2026-10-25-C names music as the last of three gaps. The operator asked Claude to prepare the amendment for approval (« Oui, prépare l'amendement set 1.11.0 … pour que je l'approuve »).

**Answers on scope**, verbatim (free text, or the label of the option chosen):
- **Source:** « Tu peux faire comme le proejst RPGcards et effectuer des musiques generes par code ». Music is synthesized by code at runtime, following the RPGCards approach.
- **Reusing RPGCards code:** « Reprendre et adapter (Recommended) ». The RPGCards music engine may be adapted to this project, with its provenance recorded in the module. This is an explicit cross-project authorization for this scope only.
- **Contexts:** « 5 pistes (Recommended) » — title, POI map, dialogue and refuge, combat, boss.
- **Default:** « Active au 1er clic (Recommended) » — on by default, but it starts only at the player's first real click or key press, never on load. Volume and mute live in the settings, remembered outside the save.
- **Scope:** « Oui, avec la musique (Recommended) ». The amendment also covers:
  - the synthesized sounds and biome ambience from the HD-2D work (OD-2026-10-29-A);
  - the same audio owner and the same limits as the current sounds.

**Additions from the independent review**, approved with the text:
