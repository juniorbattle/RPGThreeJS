## Operator checklist

- Confirm the Forest Road crop and value structure across all four surfaces.
- Confirm Kestrel identity, crisp sampling, ground anchor, and cross-surface scale.
- Confirm LEFT/CENTER/RIGHT and facing/listener evidence meets the A.4R staging doctrine.
- Confirm the strategic density is acceptable without changing camera semantics.
- Confirm the Combat Stage attack, damage feedback, and current VFX composition are visually compatible.
- If accepted, authorize Phase 4C separately; this package performs no global promotion.
