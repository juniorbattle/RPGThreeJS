# Campaign, progression and state

Status: **LOCKED**.

## Post-demo framework — OD-2026-11-07-I

Dedicated `PRODUCTION-CONTRACTS-POST-DEMO-FRAMEWORK-1` / [OD-2026-11-07-I](../autonomy/POST_DEMO_DIRECTION_OPERATOR_DECISION_2026-11-07.md), set 1.20.0, follows the operator's post-demo decision sheet. It opens the production of the campaign after the Lion demo within the bounds below. Every other locked rule stays in force, and the Lion's clauses keep governing the Lion except for the reputation calibration below.

- **Scope.** Three Seal plateaux follow the Lion in a fixed order: the Bear against the Raven, the Dragon against the Wolf, the Scorpion against the Phoenix, as §3.2 to §3.4 of the operator's narrative roadmap (`docs/content/feuille_de_route_narrative_condensee.md`) and its status describe them. The Phoenix plateau of §3.5 is retired; the Phoenix stays the Scorpion's rival clan. One final act in three parts follows: the Tournament of the Great as arena fights presented on an encounter board, the escape and the corrupted capital on a small POI map, and the final fight at the breach; then three epilogues. The game has four Seals.
- **Template.** Each plateau follows the Lion's template: three POI regions, refuges with the existing services and costs, exclusive forks with branch commitment, optional chests and encounters, challenges, stationary guardians, an honour path with two boss fights and a force path with one (§2.3 of the roadmap). Plateaux are built and delivered one at a time, and each needs the operator's acceptance before the next is built.
- **Drafts and canon.** Post-demo dialogue, choices, stages, outcomes, rewards, encounters, chests, guardians and placements are written from four sources only: the roadmap, its status, the demo's established facts and the operator's decisions. No clan, place, character, object or lore that they do not contain is added; demo facts take precedence over the roadmap. Until the operator approves a plateau, its content is a playable draft on a temporary branch from `dev`, identifiable as draft in code, and is not canon; it reaches `dev` only on the operator's order. The operator approves each plateau's texts, choices, outcomes, rewards, catalogue and placements once, as a whole. This plateau-level approval replaces, for post-demo plateaux only, the per-chest and per-elite approval of sets 1.2.0 and 1.4.0 and the rule "Do not invent new canonical choices, encounters, stages, outcomes, or rewards" below, which keep governing the Lion. Names that the roadmap leaves open or that a decision changes, such as the Scorpion chief and the final entity, come from the operator, or from proposals he validates. Until then the Scorpion chief is designated by his title, never by "Kael" or "Kaelen".
- **Reputation and clan relations.** The 0–100 reputation, its five bands and its effects carry across plateaux without reset: shop and forge prices, choice locks, credibility before a chief, refuge event weights. RunSystem owns a durable relation with each clan met, ally, neutral or enemy, set mainly by a plateau's outcome and by authored choices; the Tournament and the final act read it. Reputation and relations give no combat stat bonus.
- **Ending.** As the operator chose on the sheet (R4), the ending follows the outcomes of the four plateaux; the Lion's Serpent pursuit route counts as honour and its Lion trial route as force. Three or four honour outcomes give Respect, or Balance when reputation is below 40. Three or four force outcomes give Fear, or Balance when reputation is 60 or more. Two and two give Balance.
- **Calibration.** In the post-demo game, the Lion's reputation amounts and its reputation thresholds (recruitment at 20, witness escort at 45, Alaric's credibility at 45 and 70) are recalibrated to new values that the operator approves in the delivery package of the foundations lot before they apply. Only a perfect run reaches 100, at the end of the game; a consistent honour run reaches Renowned (80) around the third plateau. A test computes these bounds from content. The calibration changes amounts and thresholds only, never a choice, outcome, route or line, and never rewrites a saved value; a frozen demo build keeps its current values.
- **Durable facts.** Clan relations, plateau progress and outcomes, final-act progress and the chosen ending are durable facts, saved through a versioned schema with an explicit migration and resume tests. A demo save resumes at its saved point, with every clan neutral until met and no Lion fact changed. Waves, boss phases, used supports and every visual or transient state are never saved.
- **Supports and progression.** A clan support is an authored aid of an allied clan, usable once per final-act fight (COMBAT_AND_VFX). It is not progression, a level, XP or an Ultimate. Progression keeps its current channels, and Ultimates stay closed.
- **Final act.** The Serpent is always the Tournament finalist. Maelor opens the breach and forms the first phase of the final fight; an entity that stays unnamed until the operator decides forms the next. In the Fear ending, a dialogue choice after its epilogue sequence offers a last survival fight against the clans or the acceptance of one's fate. Alaric's judgement keeps its role as the Lion's climax.
