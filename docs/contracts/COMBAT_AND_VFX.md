# Tactical Combat, combat presentation and VFX

Status: **LOCKED**.

## Final audit combat rules — OD-2026-11-11-E

Dedicated `PRODUCTION-CONTRACTS-FINAL-AUDIT-1`, set 1.22.0, [operator decision](../autonomy/FINAL_AUDIT_OPERATOR_DECISION_2026-11-11.md), follows the final audit. It adds two combat rules and changes one, as exceptions to the "New rules" bullet of set 1.20.0, which otherwise stays in force.
- **Plateau scaling.** RunSystem names the fight's plateau in the combat launch request; a fight launched without one uses 1.00. The tactical runtime multiplies each foe's maximum and starting HP, and the direct damage of each hit a foe deals, by the plateau's factor: Lion 1.00, Bear 1.10, Dragon 1.20, Scorpion 1.30, final act 1.40. Scaled values are rounded to an integer, with a minimum of 1, and the damage preview shows the scaled value. Foes that enter later (a second wave, a boss phase) are scaled the same way. Damage over time, clan supports, heroes, formations, foe counts, skills, AI, rewards and the frozen demo are unchanged. The factor is transient and never saved. The five factors are this contract's values; any change to them requires a new amendment.
- **Charge break.** While a boss or an elite's intent badge shows CHARGE or ULTIME, and until it casts its ultimate or its next turn begins, whichever comes first, the runtime adds up the damage the company deals to it. Damage dealt before the badge appeared does not count, and the progress starts from zero each time the window opens. If the total reaches 15 % of the unit's maximum HP, the charge breaks: the ultimate is not cast, the ultimate cooldown returns to its full value and the badge clears, as after a skipped turn, and the combat log says so in text. While the window is open, the badge shows the break progress in text, readable without colour. A charge breaks at most once per window. The progress is transient and never saved; a resumed fight starts without it. Brisé keeps its own effect on the cycle, and Fury is unchanged.
- **Guard reserve.** For a boss or an elite, Guard counts only the AP above its cycle reserve (`bossApReserve` for its current ultimate cooldown, evaluated at the end of its turn after the Souffle): Guard is those AP, capped at 5, at +3 END per AP, and the Guard feedback text shows that amount. Every other unit keeps the existing Guard rule.
- **Truth and presentation.** The tactical runtime alone decides the factor, the break and Guard; presentation shows only resolved events, with the existing feedback text and cues and no new FX recipe, sound family or cue identity. Every other rule of this contract is unchanged, including the four-hero limit, closed Ultimates, Fury and the boss phases of set 1.20.0.

## Boss and elite Fury — OD-2026-11-11-B

Dedicated `PRODUCTION-CONTRACTS-BOSS-FURY-1`, set 1.21.0, [operator decision](../autonomy/BOSS_TEMPO_AND_FURY_OPERATOR_DECISION_2026-11-11.md), follows the operator's PC playtest. It adds one combat rule, Fury, as an exception to the "New rules" bullet of set 1.20.0, which otherwise stays in force.
- **Who and when.** A boss or an elite is in Fury while its current HP is below half its maximum HP. Fury is derived from current HP; it is transient and never saved.
- **Rule.** At most once per turn of that unit, after the first offensive action (one that targets foes to damage or afflict them) it resolves in that turn, if it is still standing and the fight is still open, the tactical runtime gives it one free basic attack, without moving, on a target in range, if one exists: it costs 0 AP, does not count toward the basic-attack surcharge, and resolves by the normal basic-attack rules (hit, critical, damage, knock-out and the +1 AP of a knock-out).
- **Exclusions.** No Fury attack on the turn the unit casts its ultimate, after a support action (healing, protection or a buff), or while it is Brisé (staggered, turn skipped), Entravé (root) or Muet (silence).
- **Truth and presentation.** The tactical runtime alone decides Fury and resolves its attack; the AI only picks its target. The attack plays the existing basic-attack presentation, with no new FX recipe, sound family or cue identity. Every other rule of this contract is unchanged, including the four-hero limit, closed Ultimates and the boss phases of set 1.20.0.

## Post-demo combat — OD-2026-11-07-I

Dedicated `PRODUCTION-CONTRACTS-POST-DEMO-FRAMEWORK-1`, set 1.20.0. The tactical runtime keeps resolving every fight, and presentation shows only resolved events. Four heroes at most; Ultimates stay closed.
- **Configs.** Each plateau's enemy families (its clans, and the fauna and corrupted creatures its roadmap section cites), guardians, challenges and bosses get their own authored configs, approved with the plateau.
- **Challenges and waves.** Post-demo challenges use the closed condition list of set 1.18.0, with the prime decided by RunSystem. Post-demo guardian fights use the two-wave rule of set 1.19.0. This extends, to post-demo fights only, the six challenge fights of set 1.18.0 and "every other fight keeps a single wave" of set 1.19.0.
- **Boss phases.** The final fight at the breach has two phases: Maelor, then the entity. When a phase's foes fall, the next phase enters by the same non-result path as a wave, with nothing restored or reset for the company. The fight is won when the last phase falls. The current phase is transient and never saved, and a resumed fight starts from its first phase.
- **Clan supports.** Before a final-act fight, RunSystem decides its supports from the saved clan relations, and the deployment states them. Each support is usable once in that fight, through the runtime's normal action legality, AP and targeting. It is an authored effect of an existing family (healing, damage, status, protection) and adds no stat system. Its use is transient and never saved. Before the fight, RunSystem adds the authored foes of each enemy clan to the formation.
- **New rules.** Any other new combat rule, including phases for another boss, is proposed to the operator, not built.
- **Sound and FX.**
  - New enemy skills get procedural FX recipes and signatures under the existing rules.
  - One synthesized ambience per new combat biome joins the closed list of set 1.11.0: Bear mountains, Dragon peaks, Scorpion desert, the Tournament arena, the corrupted capital and the breach.
  - Signature cues for post-demo bosses are proposed to the operator.
  - The closed cue table keeps its set 1.12.0 bounds of 160 identities, 32 MB and 50 ms; any growth beyond them is proposed to the operator, not built.

## Guardian waves — OD-2026-11-07-G

Dedicated `PRODUCTION-CONTRACTS-POI-GUARDIAN-WAVES-1`, set 1.19.0, follows the operator's decision sheet. The two stationary guardian fights of TRAVERSAL set 1.18.0 (`poi_elite_valley_sentinel`, `poi_elite_seal_warden`) are fought in two waves; every other fight keeps a single wave. The wave count is an optional static combat-config field, one by default, set to two for these two fights only, and never saved. When every enemy of the first wave is defeated, the tactical runtime brings in the second wave by a dedicated path that is neither the skirmish wave transition nor a result: no result card, no victory cue or fanfare, no campaign result message, and the encounter stays open. The second wave is the same annex formation with the same stats, without the QA lab's per-wave scaling, placed on the free cells nearest its annex cells; its enemies act from the next round's turn order, with existing entrances and sounds only and no new FX or sound. Between the waves nothing is restored or reset for the company: no healing, reviving, AP, status or item refill, and the round counter continues without reset; the reported facts (rounds, items used, heroes deployed) cover the whole fight. The fight is won only when the second wave falls; a defeat during either wave follows the existing defeat rules; the runtime reports the outcome once, after the last wave or the defeat. The guardians' rewards, three-hero challenge condition, prime, once-only rule and RunSystem's prime decision are unchanged, and a wave adds no reward. The current wave is transient fight state, never saved: a guardian fight resumed through the existing combat-phase continuation starts again from its first wave. The skirmish waves of the QA lab are unchanged. Presentation states the two waves before the fight and the current wave during it; it acquires no truth authority.

## Challenge fights — OD-2026-11-07-F

Dedicated `PRODUCTION-CONTRACTS-POI-CHALLENGES-1`, set 1.18.0, follows the operator's PC playtest. Six optional fights become challenges: "Meute du détour" (`lion-poi-opening-wolves`, both variants, the initiation), "Passage du troll" (`troll_crossing`), "Duelliste des représailles" (`serpent_duelist_trial`), "Nid du jeune dragon" (`young_dragon_roost`) and the two stationary guardians of TRAVERSAL. Each declares exactly one condition from a closed list, valid for the whole fight: at most three heroes, enforced by the existing deployment limit; no items, the tactical runtime refusing every item action, automatic ones included; or victory within N rounds, the tactical runtime counting rounds and reporting them with the outcome. Assignments: the initiation, the young dragon and both guardians, at most three heroes; the troll, no items; the duelist, victory within 8 rounds, a value the operator calibrates in play. Beyond N rounds the fight continues normally and only the prime is lost. A victory that holds its condition adds a challenge prime to the fight's existing rewards: two red gems, one for the initiation. RunSystem decides the prime in each of its victory paths (Lot E guardian, Lot E ordinary POI, canonical route combat) from the facts the tactical runtime reports, such as rounds, items used and heroes deployed, and from the static challenge declaration, inside the function that applies the existing rewards and before its idempotence mark; the prime is attributed once, with the same reload and duplicate-result protection, and defeat and retreat grant nothing. Round counts and the item rule are transient result and configuration facts, never saved; no saved field is added. Existing rewards, formations, enemy stats, canonical dialogue and choices, and the conduct variants that decide which elite appears are unchanged; set 1.19.0 above adds a second wave of the same formation to the two guardian fights only. Presentation states the condition and the prime on the map place that offers the fight, before deployment, during the fight (a round counter for the round condition) and on the result; it computes nothing and acquires no truth authority.

The tactical combat runtime owns turn order, action legality, AP, status application, damage, defeat, and encounter outcome. `CombatBridge` and existing campaign handoffs connect canonical combat nodes to the run. A presentation surface may observe or animate resolved events; it must not re-resolve them or change campaign truth.

Combat Stage is presentation-only. It stages canonical actors and actions and reports completion/lifecycle, but never owns a second damage/AP/status/outcome model. Preserve Character System V2 pose identities and authored combat framing. Files with historical or `legacy` names can still be production owners; inspect references before removal.

VFX are presentation-only and cannot alter an action result. Their final authored catalog is incomplete. Finish it through scoped asset, registry, visibility, timing, and reduced-motion checks tied to real resolved events. A missing effect may be documented as a production gap; it does not authorize a fabricated gameplay effect or an unrelated asset substitution.

## Sound grammar and signature cues — OD-2026-11-01-B

Dedicated `PRODUCTION-CONTRACTS-AUDIO-DIRECTION-1`, set 1.12.0, [operator decision](../autonomy/AUDIO_DIRECTION_OPERATOR_DECISION_2026-11-01.md). For this scope only, it supersedes in this contract: "it adds no family", "The operator chooses the source of each family by listening", the one-second limit for the signature cues below, and in the combat FX and synthesized-cues sections their default-off wording, read as silent until the first real gesture, and the Ultimate deferral. It authorizes sound only and does not activate, unlock or change any Ultimate. Effects stay presentation: they observe the action and never resolve, delay or change damage, AP, status or outcomes. Every other rule of OD-2026-10-19-B stays in force.

- **Composed cues.** An action cue may be composed by code from three layers: gesture, element of the FX recipe, and weight. Weight comes from the skill's catalogued AP cost and the unit's catalogued height, binned into classes in versioned code, never from current AP, HP, damage, hit, critical or target state. A cue is rendered once, ahead of play, into one buffer that uses one voice, with a PCM peak of at most 0.2 like the file candidates.
- **Bounds.** Composed and signature cues form one closed table in versioned code, of at most 160 identities and 32 MB of rendered buffers. Each is rendered in its own task under the 50 ms ceiling, and rendering never blocks mounting, input or resolution.
- **Marks.** FX recipes may carry sound marks on the FX clock. A recipe emits a mark when its clock reaches it, through the same typed allowlist and session check as the existing cues. The parent plays it on receipt or drops it: it never holds, schedules or re-times a mark. A cue that is stale or not yet rendered is dropped or plays its fallback, never awaited, and neither recipe nor resolution waits for audio. The deduplication key adds the mark index to session, event, action, phase and target.
- **Results.** A mark's identity is fixed by the recipe, independent of hit, miss, critical, damage, knock-out, target state or any random draw. Result cues are the families physical-impact, magic-impact, miss, critical, knockout, victory and defeat, and they play only when the result is applied and shown.
- **Signature cues.** The twelve Ultimates and the eight boss signatures have their own cues for charge, blow and wave, each of at most 2.5 seconds, and all signature cues of one action end within the recipe's duration plus 0.5 seconds. Every other cue stays within one second.
- **Voices.** The caps are unchanged: four voices total, two UI voices, the step cadence and no delayed queue. Signature cues use at most two of the four voices, and a signature cue is dropped rather than evict a result cue.
- **Fallback.** A composed cue plays when rendered, otherwise the file of its gesture family, otherwise silence. A signature cue falls back to the file of its blow's gesture family, or silence. The comparison between file and synthesized families is retired.
