# Narrative presentation, cinematics, Journey and audio

Status: **LOCKED**.

`STATIC_TABLEAU` is the normal interactive narrative language. Stage actors as actors: authored positions, facing, look target, depth, focus, and restaging on speaker or dramatic changes. Favor clear compositions, currently up to four visible actors. Background and cast ownership must be explicit; do not duplicate integrated video actors with sprites. Preserve dialogue text, choice agency, and canonical outcomes when changing staging.

Journey maintains campaign continuity outside playable Traversal. `TravelView` remains fallback/dev, not the principal production surface. Cinematic video is rare and reserved for these **exactly eight** production slots:

1. `camp_departure`
2. `alaric_audience_arrival`
3. `bois_clair_arrival`
4. `bois_clair_saved`
5. `bois_clair_sacrificed`
6. `lion_judgement`
7. `serpent_route_ending`
8. `lion_trial_route_ending`

These are major, grand events. `MAIN_EVENT => CINEMATIC_REQUIRED` is superseded. No default video for enemy reveals, recruits, merchants, routine travel, or minor beats. Older MP4s or slots have no production design authority; audit their references, convert the beat to the appropriate tableau/still/other authored surface, and retire media safely. Existing files or manifest entries outside the eight are **drift**, not ninth approved slots. A slot name does not certify that its current asset meets final art direction.

Use a hold only for the exact same moment established by its preceding video; release it when place, time, encounter, situation, or activity changes. Tableau and stills preserve visual-family continuity with their locations. Fallback must preserve player agency. Audio is **DEFERRED** until narrative, presentation, cinematics, and Traversal structure are demonstrably LOCKED and stable. Prepare an audio-specific decision then; do not infer a final audio doctrine now.
