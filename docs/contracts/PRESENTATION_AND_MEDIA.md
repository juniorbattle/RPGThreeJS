## Audio direction — OD-2026-11-01-B

Dedicated `PRODUCTION-CONTRACTS-AUDIO-DIRECTION-1`, set 1.12.0, [operator decision](../autonomy/AUDIO_DIRECTION_OPERATOR_DECISION_2026-11-01.md). For this scope only, it supersedes in this contract: the five-context list and the context-source rule of the code-generated music section, "Effects stay default off", the 2-second crossfade for the boss entry below, and the Ultimate audio deferral; and in the UI FX and combat FX sections, default disabled, default off, default silence and explicit enable, read as silent until the first real gesture. Every other rule of OD-2026-10-19-B and OD-2026-10-29-B stays in force, including: code-generated audio only, one parent owner, the real gesture observed in the current document, frame, origin and session validation, session close, hidden page, silent failure, preferences outside V6, and no main-thread frame over 50 ms.

- **Contexts.** Title, POI map, refuge, tension, drama, combat, boss, victory, defeat. Refuge, tension and drama replace the single dialogue and refuge context.
- **Context sources, exhaustively.** The presentation surface; the scene id and boss flag already handed to CombatBridge; for mood, the existing authored scene or surface id through a presentation-side table, never computed from route, choice, reputation, resources or outcome, and with no new field in campaign content; and for victory and defeat only, the two-valued end-of-encounter outcome, sent once by the combat document when its result card is shown as one strict typed message `{ type, sessionId, outcome }`, `outcome` being `victory` or `defeat`, one way to the parent owner under the same frame, origin and session checks, with no other field. The parent ignores a second outcome in the same session. CombatBridge's own result message is the fallback if none arrived. Music reads no other field of the result.
- **Fanfares.** Victory and defeat are fanfares of at most 8 seconds on the music stream. They start when the result card is shown, never on a skirmish wave clear and never from the `victory` effects cue. A title, map, refuge, tension or drama context requested while a fanfare plays waits until it ends or reaches 8 seconds, then enters by the normal crossfade. A combat or boss context replaces the fanfare at once by the normal crossfade or the boss-entry transition. Combat session close stops the cue voices and returns the level to Reflection; it never cuts a fanfare. A fanfare never delays the result screen, reward credit, save, defeat recovery or the return to the map.
- **Moods.** A mood changes no text, choice, stage or outcome. A surface or scene with no mood uses the refuge context.
- **Sections and bridges.** A track may be written in sections with their own tempo, key and mode. A bridge is a written section that changes tempo, key or mode and returns to the loop, inside one music stream. Sections and levels belong to that one stream.
- **Levels.** Combat music has a Reflection level and an Action level of the same track. The combat document sends one strict typed message `{ type, sessionId, level }`, `level` being `reflection` or `action`, one way to the parent owner, accepted only under the existing frame, origin and session checks. It carries no unit, skill, target, number, result or event identity, is sent where an action's presentation starts and ends, and cannot start audio before the first real gesture. The parent ignores a repeated level, applies at most one change per 500 ms and answers nothing. A level received inside that window is coalesced: the latest one applies when the window ends. The level returns to Reflection at session close, on a hidden page and after 15 seconds without an end. Music delays nothing and never blocks input.
- **Boss entry.** Entering a boss fight may use a written transition of at most 6 seconds on the music stream in place of the crossfade. Mounting the combat never waits for it.
- **Mix.** The parent owner applies one final limiter to the sum of its buses. Its -1 dBFS ceiling is measured as sample peak on an offline render of the loudest mix: four voices at peak, music and ambience at default and at maximum volume. It may lower the music by at most 6 dB, driven only by a signature cue playing: the attenuation starts with the cue and ends within 1 second after the last one.
- **Effects.** Effects, like music and ambience, are on by default and only armed: a default or restored preference never starts audio, and the first real gesture of the session does. The default applies only when no enabled value is stored. A stored false written by the effects control is honoured, a volume change alone never turns effects off, and a value stored under the former default is not a choice.
- **Ambience.** The POI map and the refuge each have one synthesized ambience loop, under the rules of the combat biome ambience. The refuge ambience plays on the refuge management surface only, not on every scene that uses the refuge context.
- **State.** No audio state is serialized in V6 or any campaign structure: context, mood, section, level, mark, cue, ambience, limiter or attenuation.
- **Deferred.** Voices, meaning speech, words and sung text, and cinematic or video audio remain DEFERRED. A wordless synthesized choir is an instrument timbre.
